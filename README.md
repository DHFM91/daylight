# Daylight

A calm, widget-style start page for your browser: clock, current weather, a 24-hour chart, 7-day forecast, air quality, and sun and moon times for any city.

**Live:** https://dhfm91.github.io/daylight/

## Features
- Big clock and greeting, plus a web search box (DuckDuckGo, Google or Bing)
- Current conditions, next 24 hours (temperature + chance of rain) and a 7-day forecast
- US AQI with PM2.5, PM10, ozone and NO₂; pollen where available (Europe)
- Sunrise, sunset, day length, golden hour and a live sun-path arc
- Moon phase, illumination, moonrise/moonset and the next full and new moon
- Background colors follow the sky at your chosen city (or pick light/dark)
- Rearrange or hide widgets; settings are saved in your browser

## Set it as your start page
Open your browser's settings, find "On startup" or "Home page", and paste `https://dhfm91.github.io/daylight/`.

## Data
Weather and air quality from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) (CC BY 4.0), no API key needed. Sun and moon positions are calculated in the browser.
